Autopilot Digital Identity Journey Tracker

Active as you browse the web. FirstNode automatically intercepts and organizes personal data submissions and OAuth sign-in events, mapping your digital footprint across an interactive timeline, network graph, and global server map.

Core Privacy Views

Chronological Timeline
Browse every data submission in sequence. Search, filter across 9 categories and OAuth, view masked values, and inspect categorized breakdowns.
Cytoscape.js Journey Graph
Visualizes your browsing journey as an interactive network graph with directional hops and time difference indicators.
Leaflet.js Server Map
Plots server locations on a global map with pulsing markers and timeline scrubber playback.
Chart.js Analytics
Track your 30-day data exposure growth, identify top OAuth providers, and export your trail to JSON or CSV.

Comprehensive 9-Category Field Detection

FirstNode automatically classifies inputs across 9 personal data categories and masks them before saving:

1. Identity Name, Username
2. OAuth Google, GitHub, Apple
3. Contact Email, Phone
4. Address Street, City, Zip
5. Government ID SSN, Passport
6. Financial Card, Bank, UPI
7. Professional Job, Company
8. Education University, Degree
9. Demographics Age, DOB
Local & Privacy-First Architecture
Your personal data never leaves your computer. Form entries are automatically masked (e.g. jo***@email.com or 41************11) and stored strictly in your browser's local storage. FirstNode contains zero external trackers or remote analytics.
